## Overview

Only 34% of new users completed onboarding and reached the core feature. We needed to understand why — and fix it.

## Research

I pulled funnel data and identified three drop-off spikes: the permissions screen, the profile setup, and the first "aha moment" gate. Exit surveys gave us the qualitative layer: users felt the app was asking for too much before showing any value.

## Solution

We restructured onboarding around a "value first" principle — show what the app does before asking for anything. Permissions were deferred until the moment they became necessary. Profile setup was made optional and moved post-activation.

## Outcome

Activation rate increased from 34% to 61% over a 4-week A/B test. Day-7 retention also improved, suggesting that users who activated with the new flow had better mental models of the product.
