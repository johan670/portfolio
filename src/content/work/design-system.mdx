## Overview

Five product teams, three different component libraries, no shared tokens. Every new feature required negotiating visual decisions from scratch. We needed a foundation everyone could build on.

## Approach

I started by auditing all existing components across products — 240 unique button variants, 18 shades of "our brand blue." The audit made the problem visible and created alignment on scope.

From there I established three layers:
1. **Tokens** — a single source of truth for color, spacing, and type
2. **Primitives** — low-level Figma components with clear props
3. **Patterns** — documented solutions to recurring design problems

## Process

Working with one front-end team as a pilot, we translated tokens into CSS custom properties and rebuilt their component library against the new foundations. Documentation was written alongside building, not after.

## Outcome

After 6 months, all five teams were onboarded. New component development time dropped by half. Designers reported spending less time on "pixel negotiation" and more time on actual product problems.
